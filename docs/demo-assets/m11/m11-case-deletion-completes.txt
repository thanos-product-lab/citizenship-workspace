WHAT THE TOMBSTONE IS
---------------------
Domain §7.4: a DELETED case retains "a minimal non-identifying deletion audit".
This row is it — that a deletion happened, to a case on this route, and when it
was asked for. Not who.

Clearing `owner_user_id` has a consequence worth seeing rather than reasoning
about: the RLS policy on `cases` *is* `owner_user_id = current_setting
('app.user_id')`, so a row owned by nobody matches nobody. **The former owner
cannot read their own tombstone.** That is the strongest available statement of
"non-identifying", and it is asserted in
`test_the_tombstone_keeps_no_name_and_no_owner`.

It is also why releasing ownership needs a `SECURITY DEFINER` function: a tenant
can never set the column the policy is made of. See ADR-0030.
