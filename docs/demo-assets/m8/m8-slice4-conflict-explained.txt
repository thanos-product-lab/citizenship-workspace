Why this capture exists
-----------------------

Every line above except the rule version was wrong before the fix recorded in
migration `0035`. The page said "All 12 travel records this assessment read were
confirmed with exact dates, so all of them counted towards the figure" directly
above a total that had excluded one; it attributed the five-day gap to "records
you have not confirmed", which the user had just confirmed; it listed the
disputed trip as "Confirmed · exact dates"; it said "No documents are linked to
these records. Every figure above rests on dates you entered yourself, not on
evidence the system has checked"; it named no fact and recorded no limitation.

All of it came from reading the stored `TravelRecordVersion`, which still says
CONFIRMED with EXACT dates because the conflict is derived, not stored
(EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LIFECYCLE_RFC §42). A result has to explain itself from what
it recorded, not from what the rows say today.

Before that, `residence.total_absences` was not even restaled when the fact was
confirmed: it stood CURRENT at 439 until some unrelated recalculation moved it
to 434.
