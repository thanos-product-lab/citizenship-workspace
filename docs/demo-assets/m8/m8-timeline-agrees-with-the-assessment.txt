Why this capture exists
-----------------------

The two surfaces above are the point. Before this change the timeline read:

  439 days outside the UK across the qualifying period ... from 12 recorded
  trips.                                  [no mention of anything held back]

  Italy   4 May 2026 to 10 May 2026   5 days      [no flag, counted]

while the Requirements page next door said 434. The timeline decided whether a
record counted by calling `counts_toward_trusted_total` on the stored row, which
still reads CONFIRMED with EXACT dates — the conflict is derived, never stored
(EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LIFECYCLE_RFC §42). One question, two answers, and the
reassuring one on the more prominent screen.

Migration `0035` could not have reached it. That migration declares rule
dependencies; a projection is a reader of the trust gate that the rule catalog
cannot see. See ADR-0028.

The copy is the other half. "Left out of your confirmed totals until you confirm
its dates" was the only held-back sentence the screen had, and for a disputed
trip it names a remedy the user already performed and that cannot resolve a
conflict.
